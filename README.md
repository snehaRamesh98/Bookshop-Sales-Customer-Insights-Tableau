# 📚 Bookshop Sales & Customer Insights | Tableau

## 📌 Project Overview

This project presents an interactive Tableau dashboard developed to analyze bookshop sales, customer ratings, author performance, publisher sales, quarterly trends, and library checkout behavior.

The objective was to transform the bookshop dataset into meaningful business insights that can support decisions related to inventory planning, marketing, author promotions, publisher partnerships, and customer demand.

## 🎯 Business Questions

The analysis focuses on the following questions:

1. Which authors generate the highest sales?
2. Which book genres perform best in terms of sales and customer ratings?
3. Is there a relationship between customer ratings and book sales?
4. Which publisher contributes the highest sales volume?
5. Which quarter records the highest sales?
6. Do books with high library checkout activity also generate high bookstore sales?

## 🛠️ Tools Used

- **Tableau Public** – Data visualization and dashboard development
- **Microsoft Excel** – Source dataset
- **GitHub** – Project documentation and portfolio hosting

## 📊 Dashboard

The final dashboard combines six analytical views:

- Genre Sales & Ratings
- Top 3 Authors by Sales
- Ratings vs Sales
- Publisher Sales
- Quarterly Sales
- Checkouts vs Sales

## 🔍 Key Insights

- **SciFi/Fantasy** is the highest-selling genre with **20,514** copies sold and an average rating of approximately **4.22**.
- **Bianca Thompson** is the top-selling author with **16,047** copies sold.
- **Etaoin Shrdlu Press** is the leading publisher with **35,609** copies sold.
- **Q3** recorded the highest quarterly sales with **22,118** copies.
- The Ratings vs Sales analysis shows an overall positive relationship between customer ratings and sales, although ratings alone do not determine sales performance.
- The Checkouts vs Sales analysis identifies books with strong library demand but comparatively lower bookstore sales, highlighting potential promotional opportunities.

## 💡 Business Recommendations

- Prioritize high-performing genres such as SciFi/Fantasy for inventory and promotional campaigns.
- Consider Bianca Thompson for author events or targeted marketing campaigns.
- Strengthen relationships with high-performing publishing partners.
- Increase inventory and staffing preparation ahead of Q3.
- Investigate books with high checkout activity but low sales and consider targeted promotions to convert reader interest into purchases.
